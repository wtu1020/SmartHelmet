EDTSNet is a lightweight time-series action-recognition network designed for high-risk operational scenarios and resource-constrained edge devices such as smart safety helmets. Unlike commonly used convolution-only models or Transformer-based time-series recognition methods, EDTSNet jointly models temporal dynamics and frequency-domain periodic patterns while preserving fine-grained local action cues. Its temporal branch employs grouped depthwise dilated convolutions in the UltraLiteBlock to extract multiscale motion features from different receptive fields at low computational cost, enabling more effective discrimination between similar actions with subtle transitional differences, such as standing and bending. Its frequency-domain branch maps inertial sensor signals into the frequency domain using LinearDCT and applies a learnable spectral-gating mechanism to emphasize action-relevant frequency components while suppressing noise caused by helmet vibration and sensor drift; a lightweight Transformer is then used to capture long-range global dependencies. Furthermore, the cross-domain gated fusion module adaptively coordinates local temporal details and global frequency-domain information, allowing the two representations to complement each other and improving both action-boundary localization and class discrimination. Experimental results on the self-built CWA dataset under identical training conditions demonstrate that EDTSNet outperforms mainstream methods from the past five years in terms of recognition accuracy and F1-score, while maintaining a lower number of parameters and FLOPs. These results indicate that EDTSNet achieves an effective balance between recognition performance, deployment efficiency, and real-time capability, making it well suited to on-device work-activity recognition on smart safety helmets with limited battery capacity, computational resources, and thermal budgets.
